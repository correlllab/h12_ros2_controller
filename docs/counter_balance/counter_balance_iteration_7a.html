Exact four-variable formulation, bounds and command path

At the 7a snapshot, real counter routed to robust3c: the frozen nominal three-component least-squares planner, using SciPy BVLS with a TRF retry, not a Crocoddyl horizon.

u contains four counter-joint velocities (shoulder pitch/roll/yaw and elbow, rad/s); the counter wrist is held. At captured task-start posture q_ref, Jc,Jm are horizontal CoM Jacobians (m/rad), Hc,Hm are roll/pitch angular momentum maps (kg m^2/rad), e is measured horizontal CoM offset (m), and omega_xy is torso-rotation-transformed IMU gyro (rad/s).

Moving-arm dq_m is the requested trajectory velocity, not measured or publisher-clipped velocity. Counter q_c, velocity and gyro come from the controller observation, which can differ from the benchmark recorder's preceding observation. With balance scale s:

bC = s (-Jm dq_m - 2 e)                [m/s]
bH = s (-Hm dq_m + 0.2 omega_xy)       [kg m^2/s; see note]
p  = -(q_c - q_ref)                    [rad/s]
min_{lower <= u <= upper}
    || sqrt(s) (Jc u - bC) / 0.1 ||^2
  + || sqrt(2 s) (Hc u - bH) ||^2
  + || sqrt(0.02) (u - p) ||^2
  + || sqrt(0.0001) u ||^2

The deployed 0.1 horizontal-velocity scale and map units are unchanged. Weights 1, 2, 0.02 are quadratic-cost coefficients: rows use their square roots, not those coefficients directly. Gyro gain 0.2 implicitly converts rad/s to momentum units; it is configured feedback, not measured inertia.

s=1 during motion and zero-duration reactive hold, then fades over 0.5 s. Posture and damping remain active at s=0, so endpoint recording includes counter posture return, not sustained balancing. Both q_ref and the horizontal CoM offset reference are captured after PREPARE; this controller does not undo preparation offsets.

The box intersects reactive limits [2.6, 3.2, 2.6, 1.5] rad/s, controller dq_lim=1, model/publisher caps, one-step joint-position room and reference-relative excursions [0.75, 0.58, 0.40, 0.58] rad. Effective free-motion caps are [0.9, 0.9, 1, 1] rad/s. Collision backtracking can multiply u by 1, 0.5, 0.25, 0.125 or zero.

q_counter_cmd = q_counter_measured + (1/30) u_applied
dq_counter_cmd = u_applied

Separate publisher clipping and gravity-torque feedforward follow. This is not integration of a persistent velocity trajectory. Fixed one-step position increments and sampled actuator response limit how modeled cancellation reaches the robot. The moving arm receives saved position/velocity targets subject to publisher caps. The logged clipped boolean does not count active optimizer constraints.

Mechanisms, offsets and measurement limits

Unbounded diagonal cancellation needs roughly 22 rad/s on a joint capped at 1 rad/s; weight-only tuning cannot remove that bounded floor. The forward-overhang posture-competition pattern also occurs in a stable FAME inner-forward case, so it cannot explain ALMI drift alone.

Across full directional banks, roughly 90% of motion states bind a solver limit. Overhang is roughly 79-81%, with smaller bounded residuals. The four sentinels have no requested-to-local-buffer velocity mismatch at the 95th percentile, but measured response is lower and delayed. DDS delivery remains unverified.

FAME sideways grows from 0.60 deg at task onset to 3.06 deg at fade end, then recovers to 1.13 deg. ALMI inner-forward starts near 5.11 deg and ends near 4.75 deg. This tuning cannot correct a large pre-existing offset. Computation overhead and zero-balance-scale posture return remain alternative explanations.

Single primary hypothesis: reduce the existing motion-phase posture weight to allow bounded forward-overhang balance action, without raising limits or replacing momentum/CoM terms. At identical recorded ALMI inner-forward states, 0.02 -> 0.002 lowers the median modeled balance residual 0.0597 -> 0.0210 and changes requested four-joint velocity by a median 0.318 rad/s. This is diagnostic sensitivity, not a simulated benefit.

Falsifier: the hypothesis fails if command differences do not reach measured counter motion, or if they do but overhang orientation/gyro or tracking fails to improve without directional/recovery regressions. It does not claim to fix the diagonal authority floor or ALMI's initial lean.

Predeclared engineering screens and simulation alignment

Reject a case exceeding any worsening threshold relative to its unchanged Counter simulation cell. These provisional engineering screens are not inferential margins:

  • Directional motion peak: over 0.1 deg.
  • Corrected timed error: over 1 mm.
  • Frozen Uniform Spatial RMS: over 0.5 mm.
  • Full-record or terminal drift: over 0.2 deg.
  • Peak gyro: over 10%.
  • Peak counter excursion: over 5 deg.

Report raw changes even inside these filters, plus speed and active-bound differences. Compare stability labels, stop/fade and counter return independently in each case. The screens only prevent a visibly unbalanced simulation tradeoff; they make no single-trial hardware precision claim.

Rate caps, realized motion and reset sensitivity

Directional requested rate-cap touches rose from 49% to 56%/59%; inner-forward rose from 14% to 30%/27%. No authority limits increased. These are rate-cap touches only, not all position/excursion-active bounds.

Saved requested and applied setpoint peaks stay within the same caps. Measured counter peaks rise only modestly: inner-forward 0.636 -> 0.648 / 0.643 rad/s. The simulated physics receiver sees a 0.9 rad/s peak buffered inner-forward command for all three settings; this does not establish real DDS receipt. Motion-to-fade transition and post-motion posture return matter more than a lower fixed posture penalty alone.

Reset sensitivity, not actuator identification: one fresh-domain repeat of unchanged ALMI inner-forward gave 0.756 deg motion peak versus 0.743 deg initially (about 0.012 deg difference), compared with candidate increases of 0.051 and 0.109 deg. A single repeat cannot establish uncertainty. Unmodeled delay/actuation could reverse a ranking elsewhere.

FAME pre-motion failures are the larger coverage limit. Do not tune MuJoCo friction or relax safety/readiness gates simply to declare a winner.

Recommendation and hardware gate

No useful tuning candidate advances from 7a. Lower posture weight improves the historical forward-overhang algebraic residual, but worsens target physical motion drift and recovery in usable ALMI simulation. FAME has no valid candidate comparison. Preserve posture=0.02.

Before any hardware-improvement claim, retain the four corrected-runtime Frame/unchanged-Counter pairs from iteration 7: eight supervised trials.

  • FAME right_diagonal_outward_up.
  • ALMI right_diagonal_outward_up.
  • FAME left_overhang_sideways.
  • ALMI right_overhang_inner_forward.

This is not candidate validation and cannot be replaced by historical old-scheduler runs. Check the external safety procedure, actual lower-policy checkpoint, state freshness, support setup, command cadence and skipped slots explicitly. No candidate comparison is justified now.

Only after a future candidate passes a complete offline screen and the unchanged corrected-runtime baseline is established should the smallest additional hardware test be a matched unchanged-Counter/candidate pair: first on the same policy and target that improved offline, then on the preserved directional sentinel for regression. Do not infer candidate improvement from Frame or historical old-runtime trials.

If overhang regression persists after that gate, the specific 7b question is how to couple feasible counter action to measured base response without motion-phase action or posture return exciting the lower-body policy. Separating movement balancing from bounded return is a formulation question, not a parameter adjustment established here.
