Why benefits are limited

  • Substantial motion for little geometric gain: median peak measured counter excursion is 33.80°, versus 0.032° for Frame's held arm. Counter reaches at least 33° in 22/40 runs.
  • Demand is not delivery: requested and locally buffered velocity reach 1 rad/s in 28/40 runs; median measured peak is 1.11 rad/s. These are different signals, not proof of motor saturation.
  • Bounds matter: reactive maxima [2.6,3.2,2.6,1.5] intersect controller/model/publisher caps to give [0.9,0.9,1,1] rad/s. clipped=false does not count active solver constraints.
  • Model cancellation is not whole-body success: robust-3C allocates four shoulder/elbow velocities to horizontal CoM and roll/pitch momentum tasks, plus posture and damping. Its SciPy solve is not a predictive DDP horizon.
  • Frame already performs well under the proxy: all cases settle under the IMU-tail criterion, with two residual-drift labels. The dataset does not measure a safe speed envelope or near-failure performance.

Across 12,005 Counter samples: 11,974 solved, 31 collision rejection/backtracking, zero post-solve clipped flags. Rejections are confined to left/right push-forward-up cases. Local buffers and predicted residuals do not establish DDS delivery or realized counteraction.

  • Primary: frozen standing-pelvis Uniform Spatial RMS to the finite desired segment, resampling the measured polyline at 1,001 uniform arc-length points.
  • Secondary: chronological spatial RMS, endpoint/hold errors and execution-clock timed tracking.
  • Base: motion drift/tilt, full peak, terminal residual and raw torso gyro norm.
  • Outcome proxy: IMU-tail stable/drift/diverging, not measured fall, contact or survival.

Frozen FK omits base rotation changes and translation. Live-rotation FK adds rotation but still omits translation. Rotation-induced wrist displacement is neither total world error nor a guaranteed lower bound: translation can reinforce or cancel it. Neither representation measures peg contact, slip, support load or world ZMP.
