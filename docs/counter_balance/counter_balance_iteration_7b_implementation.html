AR qualification: shared response model fails the relevant baseline

24 recordings and held-outs by family, arm, policy and Frame/Counter use one canonical seven-joint gain/effective-delay model, not trajectory or policy branches. Gains span 0.976-1.014 and effective delays 35-50 ms, including sampling/servo/filter effects; they are not independently measured transport delays.

Large apparent gains versus nominal dq at future target time are not the relevant test. Against the current decision-time reference at provisional 40 ms, held-out MSE improves only 2.1% for derived velocity and degrades 5.8% for reported dq. Aggregate and group screens fail. Horizon sensitivity changes the velocity screen, but no counter effective actuation horizon is independently validated. Every export remains unqualified and AR actuation is refused. Recorded ddq is not used.

Recovery is unwired

The pure recovery reference planner uses immutable commands/bounds and two-phase propose/commit with exact positive local-buffer readback. It guards cadence/freshness, finite values, position/excursion, velocity/acceleration, reference lead/stopping reserve and total PD+FF effort, and requires external validation.

Rejection, false writes, writer exceptions or clipping/readback mismatches latch STOP_REQUEST and emit no new command rather than snapping to measured position. Explicit unique-trial reset requires mode acknowledgement; external STOP cannot be cleared. The prior command can remain live until an independent lease acts. Local reference guards and frozen-measurement stopping reserve are not physical braking guarantees.

External blockers remain: full-robot swept collision checking, independent expiring command lease, mode supervision, transport/actuator acknowledgement and whole-body/effort commissioning. Synthetic coupled-inertia evidence at a 0.1 rad/s research cap is not whole-body validation; the earlier 0.2 rad/s fixture is not established feasible. No recovery connection to a live publisher or hardware enable is provided.
