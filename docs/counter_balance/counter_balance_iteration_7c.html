First finding: lower peak base tilt change, but more move sway than Frame

Diagonal and mirrored overhang

One additional cold triplet per target; values in Frame / C0 / FF-half order. On right sideways, move sway was 0.482 / 0.508 / 0.463° and full-record peak was 0.898 / 0.832 / 0.748°. The direction favors FF-half on both metrics, but there is no repeat. On right diagonal, sway was 1.119 / 1.162 / 1.100° and peak was 3.209 / 3.113 / 3.059°. Simulated C0 did not reproduce the large move-sway benefit over Frame observed on hardware. All three diagonal world-frame endpoint and hold checks failed despite joint settling.

Decision: retain FF-half as an experimental comparator. No robust sway gain over Frame is established. Test the cross-axis penalty in matched-excursion closed-loop simulation next, then isolate return timing. None of these 12 cells tested ALMI or peg contact. All 12 survived and completed, but failed the timed wrist gate. See the per-cell results and protocol and commands.

Why change feedforward?

On the November 22 left overhang, FAME move sway was 0.219° with Frame versus 0.513–0.619° with C0. ALMI Stand showed the opposite: 0.045° versus 0.014–0.027°. CoM-off did not resolve that split. Both policies showed lower diagonal sway with C0 than with Frame, but timed wrist RMS increased by about 3 mm.

Earlier fixed-state replay predicted a 0.464–0.480 rad/s RMS change in the requested four-joint counter velocity when both feedforward targets were halved, versus 0.105–0.159 for CoM-off. This predicts a distinguishable command, not a balance gain.

Sideways cancellation trades one axis for another

On the November 22 left overhang, both policies show less lateral model CoM shift with C0, but more fore-aft shift. FAME: lateral 7.66 mm with Frame versus 1.65–2.00 mm with C0; fore-aft 0.92 versus 4.67–5.43 mm. ALMI Stand: lateral 7.54 versus 1.43–1.97 mm; fore-aft 0.17 versus 3.16–4.05 mm. These are time-weighted move RMS changes from onset: rotate the recorded CoM-minus-midfoot vector by measured pelvis orientation, resolve in the initial heading, and score each horizontal component. They are not force, foot-slip or stability measurements.

On the November 10 right sideways reach, FAME Counter sway was 0.365° and 0.194° versus Frame 0.078° and 0.112° in the corresponding pairs. November 13 ALMI pairs split in direction. These separate sessions motivate a mirror test, not a pooled effect estimate.

  1. First: ff_half. Scale only the moving-arm CoM and angular-momentum feedforward components by 0.5 before allocation. Decision rule

Rank Frame, C0 and FF-half by move sway on matched reaches. Report full-record peak base tilt change and terminal lean alongside it so a quiet move cannot hide a large recovery transient. Check signed lean and torso turning for off-axis costs; wrist tracking is a secondary task check, not a controller ranking. Compare the diagonal without retuning. A single simulated triplet is a mechanism check, not evidence of repeatability or hardware clearance.

Advance only if an overhang reduction is reproducible relative to C0 and Frame, is larger than run-to-run variability and preserves directional performance, tracking and effort. If simulation reverses the measured hardware ordering, report it rather than tuning the simulator to fit.
