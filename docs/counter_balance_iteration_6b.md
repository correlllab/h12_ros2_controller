# Counter-Balance Iteration 6B

Status: **CLOSED / FINALIZED** on 2026-09-13.

## Final Question

Does the frozen robust counter-balance controller improve manipulation tracking
relative to Frame on the current rebuilt saved straight-line banks, and how does
that descriptive evidence compare with the existing challenge-group result?

The final answer is deliberately limited. The straight-line study does not show a
general geometric-path advantage for frozen 3C. It remains supporting/descriptive
evidence. The strongest existing categorical result is the separate FAME challenge
comparison: six paired improvements, zero regressions, and falls reduced from 12
to 7. This closeout does not repair `overhang_forward`, tune a controller, or run a
challenge-speed experiment.

The final analysis is
[Counter-Balance Analysis Iteration 6B](counter_balance_analysis_iteration_6b.md).
It is authoritative for results, limitations, evidence status, and the Iteration
6C handoff.

## Final Metric Hierarchy

1. **Primary spatial accuracy:** Uniform Spatial RMS, then Segment Spatial RMS.
2. **Secondary timing-sensitive tracking:** Ideal / Timed RMS.
3. **Task guards:** completion, endpoint error, projected progress, and physical
   outcome.
4. **Mechanism diagnostics:** base-induced wrist displacement, pelvis tilt, and
   pelvis translation/drift.

Uniform Spatial RMS is the arc-length-weighted RMS distance of the chronological
measured wrist polyline from the fixed desired segment. Segment Spatial RMS uses
the same finite segment but remains sample/dwell weighted. Ideal / Timed RMS
compares to the scheduled desired point at the same trajectory time and therefore
includes phase/servo lag. A spatially accurate partial path is not task completion.
Base-fixed execution RMS is a mechanism diagnostic, never a substitute for spatial
task accuracy. The precise contract is in the
[Straight-Line Trajectory Benchmark](../../../docs/straight_line_trajectory_benchmark.md).

controller, factor, attempt, run path, current bank/geometry hash, recorded physical

when an offline spatial metric is recoverable. Eight FAME
`left_overhang_forward` initialization failures lack the original stable-standing
capture; their spatial metrics remain undefined and their repair is deferred.

## Conclusions And Handoff

Iteration 6B is closed. Do not claim a general spatial-path benefit from its
straight-line grid. Do not use pooled speed cells as independent significance
samples. No controller selection or usable-envelope expansion follows from a small
spatial error alone.

**Iteration 6C:** challenge-group manipulation-speed envelope, Frame versus frozen
robust 3C, on ALMI-Manip-v2 and FAME. It starts only after a separately documented
moving-arm endpoint executability contract. `overhang_forward` repair, runtime
continuity repair, trajectory redesign, controller tuning, and the challenge-speed
experiment are deferred work, not part of the final 6B result.

## Superseded Development History

Earlier 6B drafts explored timing-gate implementation, pre-rebuild banks,
overhang-only speed sweeps, repeat overlays, and pooled-speed tests. Those steps
were useful for diagnosing the campaign but are superseded by the rebuilt-bank
inventory and finalized spatial reduction. Their lightweight provenance, summaries,
plots, classifications, and negative results are retained in the archive; the old
timed-only general-improvement and `p < 0.05` collection narratives are not final
claims.
