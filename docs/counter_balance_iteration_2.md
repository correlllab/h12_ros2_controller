# Counter-Balance Iteration 2

## Scope

This is a FAME-only design iteration for an early workshop submission. It does
not attempt to improve ALMI or produce a universal controller. The objective is
to construct an interpretable manipulation panel with at least ten distinct
frame-controller falls, then show fall-to-drift, fall-to-stable, and
drift-to-stable transitions from counter-balance control.

Experiments use the fastest validated arm profile unless a controlled pilot
shows another profile is more fall-prone. Full controller sweeps are deferred
until a small target panel demonstrates both repeatable frame falls and credible
controller recoveries.

## Model Hypothesis

MAGPIE adds distal gripper mass, length, articulated inertia, and collision
geometry. The active benchmark scenes compile as:

| Model | Total mass | `nq` | `nv` | Actuators | Geometries |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Handless | `66.984 kg` | 34 | 33 | 27 | 52 |
| MAGPIE | `67.995 kg` | 46 | 45 | 31 | 90 |

The benchmark DDS bridge controls the first 27 actuators in both scenes. MAGPIE
adds four hand actuators after that shared block. The hypothesis is that roughly
one additional kilogram of distal mass and the longer lever increase arm-induced
base disturbance enough to expose more FAME frame-controller falls.

This must be tested rather than assumed. MAGPIE also changes collision geometry,
shoulder-roll limits, and hand gravity compensation, so model comparisons must
use matched manipulation targets and report the selected model explicitly.

## Model Selection Contract

Benchmark configs use a validated top-level selector:

```yaml
model: handless  # or magpie
```

The selector controls the default MuJoCo scene and the Pinocchio assets used by
frame and reactive counter-balance controllers. Existing permanent configs state
`model: handless` explicitly. Iteration-2 configs use `model: magpie` and
`scene_h1_2_magpie.xml` so simulation, replay, and upper-controller models agree.

Target catalogs store 14 shared arm-joint configurations and are model-neutral
for playback. The generated hard and extreme catalogs record `generated_model`
and `compatible_models` separately: MAGPIE produced the discovery evidence, but
the poses are within the arm limits of both handless and MAGPIE models. Collision
validity and physical outcomes remain model-specific and must be re-evaluated
when changing the simulation model.

### Active Target Groups

Iteration-2 historical target selections now live in `data/archive/`. Active
target groups use semantic rather than experiment-specific names:

- `data/arm_reachability_targets.yaml`: 50 directional and 50 overhang targets.
- `data/arm_exploration_targets.yaml`: 20 symmetric rank-6 and named-pose
  exploration targets.
- `data/arm_hard_targets.yaml`: 12 symmetric hard targets, with two maximally
  separated shoulder-pitch endpoints in each of six physical families.

The hard group contains left and right manual-grasp, inner-upward-overhang, and
upward-overhang families. The original two hard targets are retained as lineage
metadata rather than a separate active group. The 25-target hard neighborhood is
archived as exploratory evidence; it remains the source of the completed
25-target FAME/MAGPIE result.

## Target Discovery Strategy

The first fall screen contains:

- Rank-5 targets from all ten directional families, both arms.
- Every rank from all ten overhang families, both arms.
- Both curated hard targets, with duplicate configurations counted once when
  reporting unique falls.
- Isolated left and right named poses derived from `t_pose` and
  `arms_front_45` if the existing screen yields fewer than ten falls.

Existing rank 5 is the farthest selected valid point, not necessarily a harder
new point. If the screen yields fewer than ten unique falls, farther targets are
generated by collision-checked extrapolation along the rank-4-to-rank-5 joint
branch. Joint limits, task-space extension, and collision checks remain enabled.

## Staged Experiment Plan

1. Compile handless and MAGPIE scenes and verify the shared 27-actuator contract.
2. Run a FAME/MAGPIE standing smoke test with no manipulation.
3. Compare handless and MAGPIE on a small matched fast target panel using frame
   control only.
4. Run the 62-target MAGPIE frame fall screen only if the pilot validates the
   heavier-model hypothesis.
5. Confirm selected falls in a second frame run before candidate comparison.
6. Compare current candidates on the confirmed fall panel.
7. Tune only around the best candidate using small successive-halving probes.
8. Run the final directional, overhang, and hard-target MAGPIE evaluation only
   after the small panel demonstrates useful recovery transitions.

## Current Candidate Prior

Iteration 1 found aggressive tight to be the best FAME-only candidate on valid
matched cells. It recovered the known hard fall with no matched physical
regression and retained precise tracking. Gated sustained tight matched its
physical outcome but introduced four rank-5 cross-body tracking misses and used
an asymmetric activation workaround. Iteration 2 therefore starts from:

- Frame task as baseline.
- Aggressive tight as the leading symmetric counter-balance candidate.
- Conservative reactive as a low-authority reference.
- Gated sustained tight as historical iteration-1 evidence only.

The gated sustained candidate is now retired from runnable configurations, and
the moving-arm-specific activation bypass has been removed from the controller.
Future candidates must use identical activation logic for both moving-arm sides.

Nominal and wide excursion candidates are not promoted into broad searches
unless the confirmed fall panel shows aggressive tight lacks sufficient
authority.

## Metrics

Candidate ranking prioritizes:

1. Frame falls rescued to stable.
2. Frame falls rescued to drift or stumble.
3. Candidate falls and new regressions.
4. Median and worst survivor base drift.
5. Tracking precision.
6. Foot placement and lift.
7. Zero infrastructure failures in promotion evidence.

The report distinguishes unique target configurations from duplicate catalog
IDs and presents model, motion profile, and controller candidate for every
transition.

### Matched Model Pilot

The model pilot did not show that MAGPIE weight alone changes physical outcome
classes. Both models produced the same two falls, two drifts, and four stable
trials. MAGPIE did, however, increase maximum base drift on six of eight targets.
The clearest boundary movement was `left_overhang_upward_05`, from
`0.0873 rad` handless to `0.0985 rad` MAGPIE. This remains stable under the
`0.1 rad` threshold, so it is a severity-margin observation rather than an
outcome improvement or regression.

| Target | Handless | MAGPIE | Handless drift | MAGPIE drift |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| `left_forward_outward_high_05` | Stable | Stable | 0.0229 | 0.0240 |
| `right_forward_outward_high_05` | Stable | Stable | 0.0550 | 0.0607 |
| `left_manual_grasp_fall_01` | Fall | Fall | 0.3996 | 0.4017 |
| `right_upward_arc_05` | Drift | Drift | 0.1014 | 0.1021 |

### Initial Fall Screen

The requested fast MAGPIE screen contained 62 target IDs: ten directional
rank-5 targets, all fifty overhang targets, and two hard targets. It completed
without infrastructure failures and produced 55 stable, four drift, and three
fall IDs. The right hard target duplicates
`right_overhang_inner_upward_05`, so this is only two unique fall
configurations.

The drift boundaries were:

- `right_upward_arc_05`: `0.1030 rad`.
- `left_overhang_inner_upward_05`: `0.1000 rad`.
- `right_overhang_upward_04`: `0.1180 rad`.
- `right_overhang_upward_05`: `0.1410 rad`.

Evidence:

### Farther And Named Targets

Joint-branch extrapolation generated 11 collision-checked rank-6-style targets
that extended wrist position by `0.006` to `0.093 m` beyond rank 5. Eight
isolated named targets split `t_pose`, arm-forward, arm-forward-yaw, and
arm-overhead into left and right motions. The 19-target extra-fast screen
produced 14 stable, four drift, and one fall. The fall remained in the known
right inner-upward family. Isolated arm-forward and T-pose targets were stable;
isolated overhead targets drifted.

### Hard-Target Neighborhood

The broad impulsive screen produced six fall IDs but only five unique joint
configurations because the right hard target duplicates an overhang endpoint.
To extend the hard list without changing FAME or safety thresholds, five small
collision-checked perturbations were generated around each distinct fall
configuration. All 25 perturbed joint targets fell under frame control.

These are 25 distinct target configurations but only five correlated physical
families:

- Left manual grasp.
- Left inner-upward overhang.
- Left upward overhang.
- Right inner-upward overhang.
- Right upward overhang.

This distinction matters: the workshop result demonstrates a broad local fall
basin around five failure modes, not 25 independent failure mechanisms.

### Current-Candidate Fall Panel

A balanced ten-target panel selected two neighborhood poses from each family.
Frame control fell in all ten cells. Current candidates produced:

| Candidate | Stable | Drift | Fall | Precise | Median drift |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Frame task | 0 | 0 | 10 | 4 | 0.3015 |
| Conservative reactive | 6 | 2 | 2 | 10 | 0.0925 |
| Aggressive tight | 2 | 6 | 2 | 10 | 0.1048 |
| Gated sustained tight | 6 | 2 | 2 | 10 | 0.0956 |

Conservative reactive and gated sustained tight both recovered eight falls, with
six fall-to-stable and two fall-to-drift transitions. Conservative reactive had
slightly lower median drift and does not use asymmetric activation, so it is the
FAME/MAGPIE leader. Aggressive tight was physically weaker on this stress panel,
converting six falls only to drift.

Evidence and workshop videos:

- `runs/hard_sweep/20260820_025617_fame_magpie_impulsive_fall_panel_current_candidates`.

All 25 hard-neighborhood targets were rerun in one matched frame-versus-
conservative sweep. This removes the need to extrapolate from the ten-target

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Conservative reactive | 15 | 5 | 5 | 25 |

The matched phase changes were 15 fall-to-stable, five fall-to-drift, and five
unrecovered falls. Results were uniform within each correlated physical family:

| Physical family | Stable | Drift | Fall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Left manual grasp | 0 | 0 | 5 |
| Left inner-upward overhang | 5 | 0 | 0 |
| Left upward overhang | 5 | 0 | 0 |
| Right inner-upward overhang | 5 | 0 | 0 |
| Right upward overhang | 0 | 5 | 0 |

Conservative reactive therefore recovered `20/25` correlated hard cases and
eliminated falls in all four overhang families. The left manual-grasp family is
the only unrecovered failure mode. Forty matched changed-outcome videos were

### Unresolved Manual-Grasp Family

The two manual-grasp cells remained falls for every tested existing candidate:
gain, displacement, aggressive nominal, and aggressive wide. Two new symmetric
variants increased counter-shoulder-roll excursion to `1.5` and `3.0 rad`, with
faster counter-arm motion and sustained hold. Both also failed. The variants did
improve moving-arm tracking, but maximum base drift remained approximately
`0.40 rad`.

This family commands roughly `3.33 rad` of left shoulder roll and is outside the
recoverable envelope of the current reactive formulation. It is retained as an
honest limitation rather than hidden from the panel.

Evidence:

- `runs/hard_sweep/20260820_032340_fame_magpie_impulsive_manual_fall_authority_probe`.
- `runs/hard_sweep/20260820_032753_fame_magpie_impulsive_manual_fall_extended_authority_probe`.

## Final Full Evaluation

The final evaluation used FAME, MAGPIE, the `0.5 s` impulsive profile, frame
control, and conservative reactive control. It covered all 100 directional and
overhang IDs plus both hard-target IDs, for 102 targets and 204 controller
cells.

One frame process failure at `left_overhang_sideways_05` was rerun with the
identical configuration. The successful stable, precise result and its retained
artifacts were merged into the final sweep manifest. Aggregate CSVs, summaries,
and plots were regenerated; the consolidated final directory contains 204
physical cells and zero infrastructure classifications. Merge provenance is
recorded in `arm_reachability_manifest.json`.

| Controller | Stable | Drift | Fall | Infrastructure |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Frame task | 87 | 9 | 6 | 0 |
| Conservative reactive | 98 | 3 | 1 | 0 |

Matched physical phase changes were:

| Transition | Count |
|---|---:|
| Fall to stable | 4 |
| Fall to drift | 1 |
| Drift to stable | 7 |
| Physical regressions | 0 |

The four fall-to-stable IDs include both
`right_inner_upward_fall_boundary_01` and
`right_overhang_inner_upward_05`, which share one joint configuration. The
result is therefore four catalog-ID transitions but three unique
configuration transitions. The fall-to-drift case was
`right_overhang_upward_05`. The unresolved fall was the left manual-grasp hard
target.

The seven drift-to-stable transitions were:

- `left_overhang_inner_upward_04`.
- `right_overhang_forward_05`.
- `right_overhang_inner_forward_01`.
- `right_overhang_inner_forward_02`.
- `right_overhang_inner_forward_03`.
- `right_overhang_inner_forward_04`.
- `right_overhang_inner_forward_05`.

All changed physical outcomes had precise reactive tracking. Across the full
reactive sweep, 92 targets were precise and ten were imprecise. The ten misses
were every rank of left and right cross-body motion. They remained physically
stable but had `0.136` to `1.018 rad` moving-arm error. This is a substantial
task-performance limitation and prevents claiming universal reachability
improvement.

Evidence:

- `runs/full_sweep/20260820_033117_fame_magpie_impulsive_full_conservative_evaluation`.

## Workshop Interpretation

The strongest concise result is:

- A controlled model pilot found no MAGPIE-versus-handless class-count change,
  although MAGPIE increased drift on six of eight matched cells.
- Faster arm motion, not model weight alone, exposed more FAME frame failures.
- A correlated hard-target neighborhood yielded 25 frame falls from five
  physical target families. Conservative reactive recovered 20: 15 to stable
  and five to drift.
- On the full 102-ID evaluation, conservative reactive produced four
  fall-to-stable, one fall-to-drift, and seven drift-to-stable transitions with
  no physical regression.
- The left manual-grasp family and cross-body tracking remain explicit
  limitations.

## Final Candidate

The final FAME/MAGPIE candidate is the original conservative reactive
counter-balance controller. It is preferred over aggressive tight and gated
sustained tight because it produced the best stable-recovery count, the lowest
fall-panel median drift, precise tracking on the fall panel, and no asymmetric
activation rule.

Final evaluation config:

```text
config/sweep_configs/fame_magpie_impulsive_full_conservative_evaluation.yaml
```

This promotion is specific to the FAME/MAGPIE impulsive workshop stress
condition. It does not supersede iteration-1 conclusions for ALMI or nominal
manipulation speeds.

## Moving-Arm Pass-Through Refactor

The controller implementation was moved under
`core/controller/counter_balance/` and now extends `FrameController`. The
counter objective and all symmetric candidate parameters are unchanged. Frame
IK, positional tracking, and I-stage hold own the moving arm; counter failures
hold only the counter arm.

Fast FAME/MAGPIE smoke trials covered left manual-grasp and right
inner-upward-overhang targets with frame and aggressive-tight control:

- Left manual grasp: both variants fell and tracked precisely, preserving the
  known hard outcome.
- Right inner-upward overhang: both variants drifted and tracked precisely;
  aggressive tight reduced maximum drift from `0.1128` to `0.1036 rad`.
- Reactive moving-arm velocity command error remained below
  `1.2e-7 rad/s`.
- Counter-candidate rejection occurred in the right-arm trial without blocking
  moving-arm publication.
